# Blog batch 2 — 15 posts (November 2026)

The second wave of the CANDRON engineering blog, written alongside the first 12 (see `docs/SEO.md`).
Each post answers one search a Canadian engineer, contractor, buyer or facility manager actually types.
Education comes first; CANDRON appears in at most one or two sentences in the body, plus the related links and the call to action.

## Rules every post follows

- **One searcher, one question.** The title and first paragraph answer the query directly, with no lead-in.
- **Canadian context.** Uses 600 V and 25 kV examples, CSA/CEC references and provincial utilities where relevant.
- **Structure:** a short answer up front, then H2 sections, at least one table or worked example, a `## Frequently asked questions` section (which becomes FAQ structured data), 3–4 related links and one call to action.
- **Length:** about 1,000–1,400 words.
- **Light promotion:** one natural mention of a CANDRON tool, an in-stock unit, testing service or Canadian-made option, only where it genuinely helps the reader.
- **Market updates** cite primary or named sources in a `## Sources` list, give the date of the figures, and are updated (via the `updated:` field) when the facts change.
