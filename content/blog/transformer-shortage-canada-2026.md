**As of late 2026, there are still not enough transformers to go around in North America.** Wood Mackenzie estimates that the market for power transformers is about **15% short of demand** in 2026, and substations about **8% short**. The pressure is greatest on large units of 100 MVA and up. Demand from data centres, utility grid upgrades, renewables and electrification is still growing faster than factories can add capacity. New Canadian investments will help, but most of that capacity arrives from 2027 onward. For project teams, that means planning around long lead times for at least the next two years.

*Figures in this update are current to early November 2026. Sources are listed at the end.*

Large power transformers and generator step-up units are the tightest. Distribution transformers, including padmounts and dry-types, are generally less constrained. But three-phase padmounts in the sizes used by data centres, industrial sites and renewable projects remain long-lead items. ## What is driving demand

- **Data centres.** Large new campuses need substations, padmounts and generator step-ups, and buyers are reserving factory slots well in advance. See our update on [Canada's data centre power demand](/blog/canada-data-centre-power-demand-2026).
- **Utility grid investment.** Canadian utilities have announced their largest capital programs in decades. See our update on [Canada's grid build-out](/blog/canada-grid-investment-2026).
- **Renewables and storage.** Every solar, wind and battery project needs collector-system transformers and a main power transformer.
- **Electrification and replacement.** EV charging, heat pumps, industrial electrification and the replacement of ageing fleets all add to demand.

## Supply: what is changing

### New Canadian capacity

- **Hitachi Energy, Varennes, Quebec.** In September 2025, Hitachi Energy announced a further C$270 million investment in its large power transformer plant near Montreal, which it said would nearly triple production capacity. That followed an earlier upgrade program announced in 2024.
- **Hitachi Energy, London, Ontario.** In October 2026, Hitachi Energy announced a C$479 million investment to establish Canada's first **pressboard** manufacturing line. Pressboard is the insulation inside power and distribution transformers, and its supply has been one of the industry's quieter bottlenecks.
- **CES Transformers, Markham, Ontario** opened an expanded plant in January 2026 to increase its production capacity.

These are real gains, but factory expansions take time to build and ramp up. Industry bodies such as Electro-Federation Canada continue to describe supply chains, especially for transformers, as the main barrier to grid expansion.

### US import restrictions

In the United States, Executive Order 14420 restricts imports of bulk-power equipment from certain countries, China being the largest source affected. Wood Mackenzie warned in August 2026 that this is likely to **worsen** the US shortage. Because Canadian and US projects draw on overlapping factories, tighter US supply tends to pull North American capacity south. See our update on [US tariffs and electrical equipment](/blog/us-tariffs-electrical-equipment-canada-2026) for the trade side.

## What this means for a Canadian project

1. **Order long-lead items from the single-line diagram.** Do not wait for the full electrical package. Transformers and MV switchgear set the schedule.
2. **Plan backwards from energization.** Our [long-lead equipment planner](/resources/lead-time-planner) works out order-by dates, including submittal and review time.
3. **Specify standard ratings.** Standard voltages, impedances and kVA sizes are faster to build and easier to find in stock.
4. **Ask about stock and refurbished units.** For replacements and urgent needs, [in-stock transformers](/inventory) can ship in days rather than months.
5. **Compare bids on lead time from approved drawings,** with test inclusions and price-validity terms stated.
6. **Agree price adjustment terms up front.** With copper near record highs, many quotes now carry metal escalation clauses. See [copper prices and transformer costs](/blog/copper-prices-transformer-costs-2026).
7. **Consider Canadian-made equipment.** Domestic supply avoids cross-border tariffs and delays, and makes witnessed factory testing practical.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there still a transformer shortage in 2026?

Yes. Wood Mackenzie estimates power transformers are about 15% short of demand in 2026, with large units the most constrained.

### When will the transformer shortage end?

New factory capacity is being built in Canada and the US, but most of it ramps up between 2027 and 2029. Most forecasts expect tight supply to continue until then, especially for large power transformers.

### Are distribution transformers also in short supply?

Less than large power transformers, but three-phase padmounts in commercial and industrial sizes still have long lead times. Small dry-type transformers in standard ratings are the easiest to source.

### How can I get a transformer faster?

Choose standard ratings, ask for stock or refurbished units, approve drawings quickly and order from the single-line diagram rather than waiting for detailed design.
